Deployment buckets

Named lines used in the public tracker. Amounts are end-of-month book balances.

Cash

Demand deposits and overnight balances at the concentration bank.

Money-market / short-term

Government money-market funds and T-bills with remaining maturity under 90 days.

Term deposits

Laddered certificates of deposit and time deposits, 3–12 month maturities.

Operating float / payables reserve

Balances reserved for payroll, vendor payables, and intra-month operating float.

Other deployments

Local-bank CDs, bond-proceeds holding accounts, and residual policy-eligible instruments.
